What it is

One platform for your team, your clients and your compliance

Most advice and investment businesses rely on a mix of back-office software, platform logins and email. Client Intelligence brings the everyday work together, and takes much of the routine writing and checking off your team.

For your team

Know where every client stands

A workspace with every client's portfolio, plan and history, a daily list of who needs attention, review calls prepared in advance, and reports written for you to check.

For your clients

A better service, in your brand

An app or web portal where clients see their portfolio and plan, receive personal updates and reports, message you securely, and get answers about their money at any hour.

For your compliance

Evidence without the paperwork

Every update, message, call and review is recorded against each client, with your approval before anything goes out and a Consumer Duty evidence pack ready to download.

Made to fit

Client Intelligence isn't an off-the-shelf product. We build it for each business, in your brand, connected to the systems you already use, with only the parts you want. If you need something it doesn't do yet, we can build that too.

Questions

Questions businesses ask

Can it be changed to suit our business?

Yes. Every platform is built for the business using it: your brand, your tone, the features you choose and the systems you use. If you need something that isn't on our list, we can usually build it and will quote for it separately.

Will the AI give our clients advice?

No. It explains what has happened to a client's money and answers general questions. Anything that needs advice goes to their adviser with the conversation attached, and you decide what needs approval before it reaches a client.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. We can build a new platform, or add our features to the back office and client portal you already have. Client holdings come in each night from the systems you use now.

How does it help with Consumer Duty?

Every update, message, call and review is recorded against each client, with engagement measured and an evidence pack you can download at any time.

Where is our data kept?

Securely in the UK. It's never shared or used to train AI models, and it's returned to you if you leave.

How long does it take to set up?

Usually three to four weeks from our first call to your first clients, depending on the features you choose and the systems we connect to.

Do you build websites as well?

Yes. We build new websites and refresh existing ones for advice and investment firms, at a fixed price with a small monthly fee that covers hosting, security and a set number of edits. You can take a website on its own or alongside Client Intelligence.

We already have a WordPress website. Can you move it?

Yes. We carry your existing pages across into a faster, simpler site, set up redirects from your old page addresses, and test everything on a private copy before anything changes on your live site. Nothing goes live until you have approved it.

Who is responsible for what the website says?

You are, as the firm. We build in the sections regulated firms usually need, such as your FCA register details and risk warnings, and we carry your existing wording across unchanged unless you ask us to change it. Please have your compliance team check the content before launch.
