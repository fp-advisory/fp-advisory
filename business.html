What your clients get.

A personal, plain-English view of their money — with their adviser always one tap away. Built as a new app in your name, or added to what your clients already use.

Portfolio updates

A personal update written from each client's own holdings — what changed, why it happened and how it fits the plan they agreed with you.

→ Proactive contact for every client, without anyone writing it

An assistant that knows their money

Clients ask questions in their own words, day or night, and get answers from their actual holdings and live prices. Anything needing advice goes to their adviser.

→ Fewer routine calls and emails

Reviews and reports

Quarterly reviews and annual packs written for each client — performance, income received and every charge in pounds. Downloadable as a PDF.

→ Shows clients the value of your fees

Market briefings

What moved in the markets each client follows, why it happened and what it could mean for them, written in plain English.

→ Informed clients, fewer surprises

Secure messages

A private, recorded conversation with their adviser, plus a place to add topics for their next review meeting.

→ Every conversation in one place, on record

Introductions

→ More referrals from happy clients

the Adviser Hub.

A workspace for your advisers and team. The AI does the watching, the research and the first drafts — your team makes the calls.

Who needs your attention

Every morning the AI reviews every client — portfolios that moved, worries, big market events and cash sitting unused — each with talking points and a draft message in your voice.

→ The right clients, called first

One inbox

Every client message in one place, with a suggested reply. Call requests arrive with an AI summary of why the client wants to talk.

→ Faster, better-informed replies

Reviews in minutes

Every client's quarterly review drafted from their holdings. Edit any section, add a note and approve — then publish it or send it your usual way.

→ Hours back every quarter

Research briefs

Consumer Duty evidence

→ Proof of service, whenever you need it
