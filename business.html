For your clients

What your clients get.

A personal, plain-English view of their money, with their adviser always one tap away. Built as a new app in your name, or added to the app or portal your clients already use.

Portfolio updates

A personal update written from each client's own holdings: what changed, why it happened and how it fits the plan they agreed with you. Monthly, weekly or daily — set by you, adjustable per client.

→ Proactive contact for every client, without anyone writing it

An assistant that knows their money

Clients ask questions in their own words, day or night, and get answers from their actual holdings, live prices and today's news. Anything needing advice goes to their adviser.

→ Fewer routine calls and emails

Reviews and reports

Quarterly reviews and annual packs written for each client: performance, what drove it, income received and every charge in pounds. Downloadable as a PDF.

→ Shows clients the value of your fees

Market briefings

What moved in the markets each client follows, why it happened and what it could mean for them, written in plain English.

→ Informed clients, and fewer surprises

Secure messages

A private, recorded conversation with their adviser, plus a place to add topics for their next review meeting.

→ Every conversation in one place, on record

Introductions

Clients can introduce family and friends in a tap, and the introduction goes straight to their adviser.

→ More referrals from happy clients

For your team

What your team gets:
the Adviser Hub.

A workspace for your advisers and team. The AI does the watching, the research and the first drafts — your team makes the calls.

Who needs your attention

Every morning the AI reviews every client: portfolios that have moved, worries, big market moves, quiet clients and cash sitting unused — each with talking points and a message drafted in your voice.

→ The right clients, called first

One inbox

Every client message in one place, with a suggested reply. Call requests arrive with an AI summary of why the client wants to talk, and the conversation that led to it.

→ Faster, better-informed replies

Reviews in minutes

Every client's quarterly review drafted from their holdings. Edit any section, add a personal note and approve, then publish it or send it your usual way.

→ Hours back every quarter

Adviser research briefs

For every opportunity: the evidence, key figures, what the market expects, target ranges, the risks and which of your clients it may suit.

→ Better client conversations, without a research team

Consumer Duty evidence

Engagement and understanding measured for every client, with a dated service record and an evidence pack ready to download whenever you need it.

→ Proof of service, whenever you need it

AI assistant for your team

Ask about any client, investment or review in plain English. Answers from live data and your clients' holdings, drafts messages, summarises positions and prepares you for meetings.

→ Answers in seconds, not hours
