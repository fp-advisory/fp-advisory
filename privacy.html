Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

FP Advisory ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Note: This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Use and Risk Disclaimer.

1. Who we are

FP Advisory operates at fp-advisory.co.uk and app.fp-advisory.co.uk. For data protection enquiries, contact us at info@fp-advisory.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register for a free trial or subscription.
  • Communication data — messages you send us via the contact form.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app and website, including pages visited, features used, and session duration.
  • Trading account data — if you connect a third-party brokerage account (e.g. via cTrader), we receive only the data necessary to place and manage trades on your behalf. We do not store your brokerage credentials.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Push notification tokens — if you opt in to push notifications via the app.

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide and maintain the FP Advisory service.
  • Send you trade ideas, alerts, and account notifications.
  • Process subscription payments and manage your account.
  • Respond to your support enquiries.
  • Improve the service through analytics and usage monitoring.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — to deliver the service you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our service and communicate service updates.
  • Consent — for optional features such as push notifications, which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where required by law.

5. Third parties we share data with

We may share your data with the following third-party service providers:

  • cTrader / your brokerage — to execute trades on your behalf when you use the in-app trading feature.
  • Finnhub — for market data (no personal data shared).
  • Formspree — to process contact form submissions.
  • Netlify — for website and function hosting.
  • Titan Email — for email delivery.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, or as required to fulfil the purposes set out in this policy. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your data within 90 days unless we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to processing or request restriction of processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis for processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@fp-advisory.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary for the website and app to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No cookie consent banner is therefore required under UK guidance for strictly necessary cookies.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or via the app. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
