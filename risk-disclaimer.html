2. Risk of loss

Trading forex, contracts for difference (CFDs), and other financial instruments carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. The value of financial instruments can fall as well as rise, and you may lose more than your initial deposit. You should carefully consider your financial situation, level of experience, and risk appetite before trading.

Specific risks include:

  • Market risk — prices can move rapidly and unpredictably in response to economic, political, and other events.
  • Leverage risk — many forex products involve leverage, which amplifies both profits and losses.
  • Liquidity risk — in some market conditions it may be difficult to execute a trade at the expected price.
  • Execution risk — there may be delays or failures in trade execution, particularly in fast-moving markets.
  • Technology risk — the FP Advisory app and in-app trading feature depend on third-party infrastructure, which may experience outages or errors.
  • Model risk — our AI systems may generate incorrect or misleading trade ideas. All AI output is reviewed by a human before publication, but errors can still occur.
