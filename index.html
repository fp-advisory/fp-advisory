How it works

From noise to conviction.

Every idea goes through the same three-stage discipline, start to finish.

Step 01: Scan

It never stops watching

Around the clock, the system reads price action and macro signals across every major currency pair and metal, watching most closely around the London and New York opens where the real moves happen.

Step 02: Filter

Only the real stuff survives

A minor wobble with no technical backing gets thrown out on sight. What's left gets built into a full case: what happened, why it matters, and what we expect next.

Step 03: Validate & review

Stress-tested, then signed off

A second, independent AI looks for a reason to kill the idea. Whatever survives gets a final look from a professional, approved or rejected individually, every time.
